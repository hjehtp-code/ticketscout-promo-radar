Knott's 2027 Pass: Renewal Friend-Ticket Dates

Keep the renewal bonus separate from the 2027 pass. The official footnote says renew by November 1, 2026 for one free friend admission at your home park on a public operating day through the 2026 season. It does not establish friend-ticket use in 2027 or eligibility for a first-time buyer.

Checked 2026-10-04 · Knott's 2027 Season Pass / Gold Pass renewal-bonus wording; no assumed redemption process or complete renewal eligibility.

The renewal footnote changes the reading

The official Knott's season-pass page advertises 2027 passes and displays a visit-period row covering 2026 and 2027. Its Season Pass and Gold Pass cards separately advertise a free Bring-A-Friend ticket for renewing by November 1. In the public View Details panel, the renewal footnote specifies November 1, 2026 and describes one free friend admission at the home park on a public operating day through the 2026 season.

Those are different periods. The bonus footnote does not grant a friend visit throughout the 2027 pass year. The same panel says the issuing home park must be visited first before other parks, and excludes Knott's Scary Farm, special ticket events and park buyout admission from the stated pass admission. Product benefits may change and vary by park. We have not established bonus issuance timing, an online redemption procedure or every renewal-eligibility condition.

TicketScout's three-clock test

Draw three separate timelines in your own planning notes. The first is the passholder's admission period. The second is the deadline to make a qualifying renewal purchase. The third is the time when the friend bonus can actually be used. Assign each date from the official product description to one timeline before deciding whether the offer fits. A date belonging to one benefit cannot fill an unanswered field for another benefit.

Separate a new buyer from a renewing buyer

Make a buyer-status column before putting the promotion into your plan. Mark a first purchase as new, and a purchase replacing an existing pass as a proposed renewal. The word proposed matters until the operator confirms which existing pass and purchaser qualify. A renewal headline alone cannot establish that someone buying a first pass receives the same bonus.

Use a second column for the benefit being assessed: ordinary pass admission, a friend discount, or the specific free renewal bonus. These are different claims. A general friend-discount label does not prove a free ticket, while a free renewal bonus does not determine every future friend-discount condition. Keep each claim attached to its exact source wording and purchaser status. This prevents two separately advertised benefits from becoming one imagined entitlement.

Apply the home-park gate

Record the issuing park in the plan, then record the first park you intend to visit. The reviewed detail panel requires a first visit at the home park before visiting other parks. A region-wide admission label therefore needs a sequence check, not just a destination check. If your first planned stop is elsewhere, resolve that sequence before counting the pass as suitable for the itinerary.

Next record the friend's destination independently. The renewal bonus specifies the home park; the broader admission territory attached to the passholder does not automatically broaden that friend bonus. Two people travelling together can have different admission rights. Check them separately and mark a destination mismatch instead of assuming the passholder's regional coverage carries the friend along.

A go, hold or reject decision for the bonus

Use go only when buyer eligibility, renewal deadline, intended friend-use period, home park and the required public operating day all match confirmed terms. Use reject when a stated condition conflicts with your plan. Use hold when the outcome depends on an unanswered question, such as when the free ticket becomes available or how it is redeemed. These labels describe the proposed bonus use, not whether the entire pass is worth purchasing.

Before relying on the bonus, ask the operator about the exact hold field. For example, ask whether a qualifying renewal made on your intended date issues the friend ticket in time for your planned public operating day. Save the answer with the relevant product terms. Do not convert a hold into go simply because a promotion deadline is approaching.

Keep the pass decision independent

If the friend bonus fails your plan, reassess the pass using the visits and benefits that remain confirmed for you. An unusable bonus has no demonstrated cash value in that itinerary. Conversely, a usable bonus is not automatically a cash saving unless it replaces a purchase you actually intended to make. We have not computed a break-even visit count or a family total: that would require the exact alternative ticket prices, selected visits and applicable charges.

Questions before choosing a 2027 pass

Does the 2027 label make the renewal friend bonus valid in 2027?

No such entitlement is established by the reviewed footnote. It limits the described bonus to public operating days through the 2026 season.

Does a first-time buyer automatically get the renewal bonus?

The offer is framed as a renewal. We have not verified eligibility for a new buyer; ask the operator rather than assuming it applies.

Can the friend bonus follow me to another regional park?

The reviewed bonus footnote names the home park. It does not establish transferable regional use for that bonus.

Can I rely on the pass for Knott's Scary Farm?

The reviewed admission footnote excludes Knott's Scary Farm. For single-visit event and parking distinctions, see our separate admission guide.
