LEGOLAND Florida: Under-Two Ticket Age Check

Children under two receive free admission to LEGOLAND Florida; ages two and above need a theme park ticket. Check age for the planned visit date. The park’s recommended age range is separate from this ticket rule.

Checked 2026-10-16 · Florida theme-park admission age only. Independent local date tool; no price, ride entitlement or booking verified.

The official admission boundary

The official Florida ticket FAQ, checked October 16, 2026, states that children younger than two receive free admission and children aged two or older need a theme park ticket. Its separate age-suitability answer describes a park geared toward ages two to twelve; that recommendation does not replace the admission rule.

The reviewed answer does not establish age-document requirements, special birthday exceptions, every add-on entitlement or a February 29 convention. Confirm unresolved details with the operator.

Check a birth date against a visit date

This independent planning tool compares calendar dates. It runs locally, without submitting or saving its date inputs. Avoid entering a name or other identifying details.



Choose both dates to check.

Use the visit date, then repeat for each day

Start with the actual birth date and each date on which the child will enter the park. Do not substitute the booking date, the date you read a promotion or the start of the school year. The tool identifies whether a visit falls before the second birthday or on or after it. It does not reserve a ticket or decide how an operator will process an existing booking.

For example, consider a hypothetical child born October 20, 2024. October 19, 2026 falls before the second birthday; October 20 and October 21 do not. These are date comparisons, not sample ticket prices. If a trip spans that birthday, check each planned entry separately and ask the operator how the selected multi-day product must be arranged. Do not assume the age on the first day fixes every later day's entitlement.

Separate eligibility from the product you buy

The output answers a narrow age-boundary question. It does not select an admission package, establish a discounted price or tell you whether a hotel offer includes a child's ticket. Once a ticket is required, inspect the actual selected product and the attractions it covers. If a package has its own age or party conditions, record those separately rather than replacing the general boundary with the package's marketing description.

For a household, make one line for each child and each visit date. Record the date comparison first and the intended admission product second. This exposes a missing ticket before you combine the party into a single budget. Do not calculate a party total until the selected products and charges are confirmed. No saving, age-based ticket price or promotional entitlement is calculated here.

Do not confuse admission with ride suitability

Being admitted and being eligible for a particular ride are different decisions. A recommendation about the park's intended audience does not establish a ride's height requirement, supervision rule or availability. Keep a separate attraction checklist and read its actual requirements before promising that a child can participate. This guide does not verify those attraction-specific rules.

Similarly, a child outside the recommended audience range does not gain a free-admission exception from that recommendation. Use the explicit admission answer for the ticket question, then decide whether the planned experience suits your family. Do not transfer an age rule remembered from another LEGOLAND resort to Florida without checking that resort's own current guidance.

Handle date changes and unresolved cases openly

If the visit moves, run the comparison again before relying on the old result. The calculator refuses invalid dates and a visit before birth; for a February 29 birth date, it asks for operator confirmation instead of choosing a birthday convention. These safeguards are limits of this independent tool, not additional park policies.

Keep the confirmed rule and selected product together with your booking notes. If an existing order was made before the birthday for use afterward, ask the operator what action is needed. We have not established an exchange process, refund right or grandfathering exception. Do not infer one from the fact that the original purchase occurred while the child was younger.

Questions and answers

Does “under two” include a child who has turned two?

No. The Florida ticket FAQ places ages two and above in the ticket-required group.

Does the tool issue free admission?

No. It compares dates against the published boundary. It does not issue or reserve any entitlement.

What if my booking spans a birthday?

Check each visit date and confirm the exact multi-day product with the operator; the reviewed FAQ does not settle that booking procedure.
