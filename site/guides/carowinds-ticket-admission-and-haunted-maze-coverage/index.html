Carowinds Tickets: Park Admission, Haunted Mazes and Separate Experiences

Match your Carowinds ticket to the access you need. The SCarowinds Bundle lists park admission plus haunted mazes; the separate Haunted Attractions Pass excludes park admission. The Conjuring is listed as a separate experience. Confirm your exact products and chosen night before paying.

Checked 2026-10-02 · Carowinds official daily-ticket and SCarowinds add-on descriptions; other parks and products are outside this comparison.

What the official products separate

The official daily-ticket page lists a Park Ticket with admission on the selected date and fall events on selected dates. It separately lists a SCarowinds Bundle combining park admission and haunted-maze access. Those are different coverage descriptions.

The official add-ons page lists a Haunted Attractions Pass for maze access on the selected night and expressly excludes park admission. The Conjuring: Beyond Fear is a separately listed experience, also on the selected night and excluding park admission. We have not established that every maze pass or bundle includes that separately named experience.

Build a coverage row before comparing prices

Write down what each guest actually wants to enter: the park, haunted mazes, and any separately named experience. Put those three needs in separate columns. A product name that contains the event name is not a substitute for a filled coverage row. For each product, mark only the rights that its own description explicitly grants. Use unknown for an unanswered field rather than treating silence as either included or excluded.

Next put the chosen visit date beside every row. An admission ticket and an add-on can each be valid yet fail to match your planned night. Check them together before comparing prices. Keep evidence links next to the cells so that another member of your party can see which statement supports the decision without rereading every page.

Two purchase paths to compare

If you already hold suitable park admission, begin with that existing right. Identify which desired experiences remain uncovered, and compare the appropriate add-on choices. Do not buy a second admission merely because a bundle appears convenient. If you have no admission, begin with complete paths: a suitable admission product plus the needed extras, or a bundle that explicitly covers those same needs.

A bundle and a maze-only product are not competing prices for the same thing. Eliminate incomplete paths first. Then compare the remaining paths for the same guests, night and desired attractions. Keep an already-paid ticket separate from new spending, and label your comparison accordingly. A sunk purchase and a new purchase answer different budget questions.

A party worksheet without an invented total

Use one row per guest. Record admission already held, admission still needed, maze access still needed and separately named experiences still needed. Add parking for your actual vehicle and the final applicable taxes and processing charges. Do not enter zero when a charge is unreadable. Our comparison is incomplete until every necessary input is known.

Calculate new spending as the sum of the actual selected products plus the applicable checkout charges and required parking. A headline starting price is not a quote for your night or every guest. Compare totals only after the selected date and guest eligibility have been confirmed. Keep optional queue upgrades in their own column: reducing a wait is a separate decision from obtaining access.

Stop checks before payment

Read the final product names back against your coverage row. Confirm that all purchases refer to the intended location and night. Check whether an experience is named separately, whether a queue upgrade grants the access you need, and whether your existing admission really applies. Save the relevant product terms with your receipt. When an unresolved field determines whether you can enter, ask Carowinds about that exact field before relying on the purchase.

Questions before choosing Carowinds tickets

Does a Haunted Attractions Pass include park admission?

No. The reviewed official product description excludes park admission.

Is the SCarowinds Bundle the same as a maze-only pass?

No. The bundle explicitly lists park admission and maze access, while the separate Haunted Attractions Pass excludes admission.

Does a maze pass prove access to The Conjuring?

We cannot establish that from the reviewed descriptions. The official page lists The Conjuring separately; confirm the exact product before relying on that access.

Can this guide quote my family’s final cost or refund rights?

No. We have not verified date-specific checkout prices, all guest eligibility, required parking or complete refund and change terms. Those fields remain unresolved.
