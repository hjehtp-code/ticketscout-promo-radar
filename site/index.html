LEGOLAND Ticket: Check Inclusions and Expired Offers Before You Buy

Choose the exact LEGOLAND location and ticket first, then check its included parks and visit dates. For Florida, the official ticket page includes SEA LIFE with current theme-park admission, but an old BOGO terms block on the same page describes visits ending August 16, 2026. That block does not establish a current BOGO deal.

Carowinds Tickets: Park Admission, Haunted Mazes and Separate Experiences

Match your Carowinds ticket to the access you need. The SCarowinds Bundle lists park admission plus haunted mazes; the separate Haunted Attractions Pass excludes park admission. The Conjuring is listed as a separate experience. Confirm your exact products and chosen night before paying.

Knott's Berry Farm Tickets: Daytime, Scary Farm and Parking

Choose admission for your intended session: the daytime Park Ticket does not establish Scary Farm entry. Scary Farm Fright & Fast Lane excludes event admission, and the listed Daily Parking excludes Scary Farm. Confirm event entry and event-valid parking before comparing your total.

Knott's 2027 Pass: Renewal Friend-Ticket Dates

Keep the renewal bonus separate from the 2027 pass. The official footnote says renew by November 1, 2026 for one free friend admission at your home park on a public operating day through the 2026 season. It does not establish friend-ticket use in 2027 or eligibility for a first-time buyer.

Knott's Membership: Monthly Payment and Minimum Term

Knott's monthly membership has a minimum 12-month term with 12 equal monthly payments plus an initiation fee, then renews monthly. Regular's listed $10 monthly rate and $30 initiation fee calculate to $150 before taxes and unverified charges, not a final checkout quote. Cancellation instructions remain unverified here.

Carowinds Fast Lane: Daily, All Season and Haunted Access

Daily Fast Lane covers one visit on selected ride queues; All Season covers repeated visits under its passholder terms. Daily excludes park admission; All Season is a passholder add-on, so confirm admission separately. Fast Lane does not include SCarowinds Haunted Attractions: verify that separate pass before buying.
